Oil & Gas Tycoon original oilfield art

The pumpjack, heavy pumpjack, drilling rig, gas gathering skid, field tanker,
and heavy tanker models were authored for this Pipeapp game project. Their
geometry uses no downloaded meshes or textures. Source is retained in
art_sources/sources/original_equipment.py.

The district terrain, pipelines, map controls, and illustration surfaces are
also original project code. These original works are separate from the
Kenney CC0 assets; this notice makes no additional license grant.
