Pipe Buyer

Buy & Sell Marketplace for Oil & Gas Products Globally

Application Purpose & Functionality

Pipe Buyer (accessible at https://www.pipebuyer.com) is an international B2B industrial marketplace, timed auctions platform, escrow settlement provider, and freight trucking dispatch network for buying and selling oil & gas pipe, OCTG casing, tubing, line pipe, valves, and heavy equipment.

Businesses use Pipe Buyer to:

  • Publish industrial equipment and pipe inventory listings with technical specifications.
  • Search and locate equipment globally using interactive map and filter tools.
  • Communicate directly between verified buyers and sellers with attachment support.
  • Participate in timed auctions with verified commercial bids.
  • Arrange trucking dispatch and utilize transaction-protection escrow features.

Google Sign-In & Data Usage

Pipe Buyer uses Google Sign-In to authenticate users and create their Pipe Buyer account using their name, email address, and profile photo.

  • We access your Google email address, full name, and profile photo solely to establish your user identity.
  • Google user data is stored securely using Firebase Authentication protocols.
  • Google user data is never sold, rented, or used for advertising purposes.
