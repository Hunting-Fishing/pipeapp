Pipe Buyer

Buy and Sell Oil and Gas Pipe, Equipment, and Industrial Assets

Application purpose and functionality

Pipe Buyer is an international B2B industrial marketplace for buying and selling oil and gas pipe, OCTG casing, tubing, line pipe, valves, heavy equipment, portable buildings, crew vehicles, and related industrial assets. The platform also supports timed auctions, buyer and seller messaging, map-based discovery, transaction-protection workflows, and freight trucking dispatch.

Businesses use Pipe Buyer to:

  • Publish industrial equipment and pipe inventory listings with technical specifications.
  • Search and locate equipment using interactive maps, location data, and filters.
  • Communicate directly with verified buyers and sellers.
  • Participate in timed auctions and submit commercial offers.
  • Arrange trucking dispatch and use transaction-protection features.

Google Sign-In and Google user data

Pipe Buyer uses Google Sign-In only to authenticate a user and create or access that user's Pipe Buyer account.

  • Pipe Buyer requests the user's Google email address, full name, and profile photo.
  • This information is used to establish account identity and display the user's Pipe Buyer profile.
  • Google user data is stored through Firebase Authentication and is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.
