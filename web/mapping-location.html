Mapping and Location Policy for Pipe Buyer

Effective Date: August 22, 2026
Last updated: August 22, 2026

This policy explains how Pipe Buyer uses map pins, entered addresses, pickup and delivery locations, service areas, and device location in Marketplace and Dispatch features.

Maps are planning tools, not legal routing instructions. Distances, travel times, route lines, geocoded addresses, and pins may be approximate or wrong. Commercial operators must independently verify legal access, road conditions, permits, bridge and weight restrictions, dimensions, dangerous-goods requirements, and other route obligations.

1. Location Information Used by Pipe Buyer

Pipe Buyer may process locations that you enter for listings, wanted ads, Dispatch pickup or delivery, service areas, profile regions, or searches. If you grant device-location permission, Pipe Buyer may also use approximate or precise device location to support nearby discovery, map centering, pin placement, distance estimates, or related location features.

2. Permission and User Control

Device location is used only through the permissions and controls provided by your browser or operating system. You may deny or revoke that permission. Some location-aware features may become less useful or may require manual address or region entry when location access is unavailable.

3. Public and Restricted Location Details

Do not publish sensitive access details that should be shared only with a transaction participant. Pipe Buyer may present a general or approximate location publicly while keeping more specific Dispatch access information within the job workflow where the product supports that restriction. Users remain responsible for the information they intentionally enter or send.

4. Pins, Geocoding, and Address Accuracy

A displayed pin can differ from the real entrance, yard, lease road, legal land description, shop, loading point, or delivery location. Before travelling, loading, dispatching, or completing a transaction, confirm the destination directly with the applicable user and verify site access.

5. Route and Distance Estimates

Route, mileage, ETA, and distance estimates may be generated from third-party or open mapping data and may not account for seasonal roads, private roads, closures, construction, ferries, road bans, bridge restrictions, low clearances, axle limits, oversize/overweight requirements, weather, or commercial-vehicle restrictions.

6. Carrier and Driver Responsibilities

Carriers and drivers are solely responsible for legal routing, operating authority, licensing, permits, hours-of-service compliance, commercial vehicle restrictions, cargo securement, dangerous-goods requirements, and safe operation. Pipe Buyer is not a carrier, dispatch authority, mapping authority, or substitute for a professional route survey or permit service.

7. Location Safety

Use caution when sharing home addresses, remote-site coordinates, gate codes, access instructions, or other sensitive details. Where practical, exchange exact access details only with the party who needs them for an awarded job or active transaction. Report suspicious requests for unnecessary location information.

8. Fraud and Location Misrepresentation

You must not falsify a listing, cargo, pickup, delivery, business, or equipment location; impersonate another site or company; manipulate a pin to mislead users; or use location features to facilitate theft, trespass, stalking, fraud, or other unlawful conduct.

9. Data Handling

Location information is handled under the Pipe Buyer Privacy Policy. Depending on the feature, location information may be stored with listings, Dispatch records, messages, security records, or transaction history for operational, fraud-prevention, dispute, accounting, audit, or legal purposes.

10. Reporting Mapping Problems

If a pin, address, or route is materially wrong or creates a safety concern, use the available reporting/support tools or contact support@pipebuyer.com. Include enough non-sensitive information for Pipe Buyer to identify the affected listing or Dispatch job.

11. Changes to This Policy

Pipe Buyer may update this policy as mapping providers, location features, jurisdictions, and safety controls change. A material change may require review and acceptance of the current version before protected Marketplace or Dispatch activity continues.
