Privacy Policy for Pipe Buyer

Effective Date: August 22, 2026
Last updated: August 22, 2026

Pipe Buyer, available at https://www.pipebuyer.com, is a business-to-business marketplace, timed-auction platform, messaging service, and freight dispatch network serving users in the United States and Canada.

Payment distinction. Pipe Buyer may collect subscription fees and other fees owed directly to Pipe Buyer through Stripe or another approved provider. Payment card numbers, CVV, and bank credentials are collected by the payment provider, not stored by Pipe Buyer. Unless a separately disclosed marketplace payment feature is activated, Pipe Buyer does not operate escrow or take custody of buyer-to-seller settlement funds.

2. Stripe and Payment Processing

For the current approved web-based Dispatch billing flow, Stripe processes payment information. Stripe may collect billing address, payment method information, payment authentication information, device/fraud signals, and other data required to complete or manage a payment under Stripe's own privacy practices.

Pipe Buyer sends Stripe the server-selected plan or price, account reference, billing-purpose metadata, and other limited transaction information needed to create, secure, reconcile, refund, dispute, or manage the payment. Pipe Buyer receives provider status and identifiers needed to determine whether a Dispatch membership has actually been paid.

A browser return page is not treated as payment evidence. Pipe Buyer relies on authenticated provider events and server-side reconciliation before establishing paid access.

3. Use of Google User Data

  • Data accessed: When you use Google Sign-In, Pipe Buyer requests your Google email address, full name, and profile photo.
  • Purpose: Authentication, account creation, and profile display.
  • Use and sharing: Google user data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or used for AI model training.

Pipe Buyer's use and transfer of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

4. How We Use Information

  • Create, authenticate, secure, and administer accounts.
  • Publish and operate listings, wanted ads, offers, auctions, messaging, and Dispatch.
  • Create and manage approved paid features, subscriptions, promotions, refunds, disputes, and reconciliation.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, account compromise, duplicate content, unsafe communication, and policy violations.
  • Provide notifications, support, moderation, appeals, and operational records.
  • Comply with tax, accounting, payment, legal, security, and record-retention obligations.
  • Diagnose reliability and performance issues.

Pipe Buyer does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

5. Automated Signals and Human Review

Pipe Buyer may use conservative automated signals to detect duplicate images, suspected fraud, unsafe messages, account compromise, payment risk, or other abuse. Automated signals may create a private review case. Material moderation decisions are subject to human review and an appeal process where one is provided.

6. Data Sharing & Service Providers

Other users. Information you intentionally publish or send to another user is visible to the applicable audience. Exact Dispatch access details are restricted to users who need them for the awarded job where the product supports that restriction.

Google Firebase / Google Cloud. We use Firebase and Google Cloud services for authentication, databases, storage, server functions, messaging, diagnostics, security, and hosting.

Stripe. We use Stripe for approved payment, Billing, subscription-management, refund, dispute, fraud-prevention, and reconciliation functions. Stripe processes payment credentials directly.

Legal and safety. We may disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, Pipe Buyer, property, platform security, or the public.

Business transfer. Information may transfer as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganisation, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

7. Where Information Is Processed

Pipe Buyer's production application data is primarily stored in Google Cloud infrastructure in the United States. Payment providers and other service providers may process information in additional locations under their applicable contracts and legal obligations. Canadian users should understand that information processed in the United States may be subject to lawful U.S. access requests.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete certain information and to object to or restrict certain processing. Pipe Buyer provides account, profile, session, notification, location, export, and deletion controls where available in the application. Contact us for requests that cannot be completed in the app.

Users in Canada may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. Users in U.S. jurisdictions with applicable privacy laws may have additional statutory rights.

10. Security

  • Firebase Authentication and account verification controls.
  • Multi-factor authentication for administrator accounts.
  • Server-side authorisation for authoritative marketplace, Dispatch, moderation, and payment state.
  • Firestore and Cloud Storage security rules.
  • Firebase App Check and rate limiting.
  • Signed Stripe webhook verification and idempotent payment processing.
  • Audit records for administrative, policy, payment, and moderation actions.

No system is perfectly secure. Where required by law, we will notify affected users and regulators of qualifying security incidents.
