Privacy Policy for Pipe Buyer

Effective Date: August 3, 2026

Application Overview: Pipe Buyer (accessible at https://www.pipebuyer.com) is an online B2B industrial marketplace, timed auctions platform, escrow settlement provider, and freight trucking dispatch network for buying and selling oil & gas equipment, OCTG casing, tubing, line pipe, valves, and heavy machinery globally.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, list products, or perform transactions on Pipe Buyer, we collect personal and business information necessary to deliver our marketplace services, including:

  • Account registration information (name, business email address, company name, phone number).
  • Transaction data (listing details, bids, purchase orders, shipping address).
  • Authentication data via Google Sign-In and email authentication.

2. Google User Data Policy & Disclosure

Use of Google OAuth & User Data: When you use Google Sign-In with Pipe Buyer, we access basic profile information provided by Google OAuth APIs (specifically your name, email address, and profile photo URL) solely for authenticating your identity and displaying your account profile.

  • Pipe Buyer does not sell, rent, or lease Google user data to third parties.
  • Pipe Buyer does not use Google user data for advertising, marketing, or behavioral tracking purposes.
  • Pipe Buyer does not use Google user data to train generalized AI or machine learning models.
  • Google OAuth data is used strictly to establish and secure your user session within the Pipe Buyer application.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected data exclusively to:

  • Provide, maintain, and secure the Pipe Buyer marketplace, escrow settlement, and freight dispatch network.
  • Process buying, selling, and bidding operations requested by users.
  • Verify user identities and prevent fraud or unauthorized access.
  • Communicate order updates, freight tracking status, and account notifications.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Pipe Buyer shares data only as required to complete marketplace transactions (e.g., sharing delivery addresses with freight carriers for dispatch, or processing payouts via verified banking escrow providers). We do not share personal data with unauthorized third parties.

5. Data Security & Retention

We employ industry-standard encryption, SSL protocols, and Firebase Security Rules to protect user information. Account data is retained as long as your account remains active or as required by law.
