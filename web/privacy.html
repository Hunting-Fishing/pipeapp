Privacy Policy for Pipe Buyer

Effective Date: August 3, 2026
Last updated: August 8, 2026

Application Overview: Pipe Buyer (accessible at https://www.pipebuyer.com) is an online business-to-business marketplace for industrial equipment and materials, including oil & gas equipment, OCTG casing, tubing, line pipe, valves, and heavy machinery. Pipe Buyer provides listings, wanted ads, offers, timed auctions, messaging, and a freight dispatch network that connects customers with independent carriers. Pipe Buyer currently serves users in the United States and Canada.

Pipe Buyer is a venue, not a party to your transaction. Pipe Buyer does not hold or release funds, does not operate escrow or trust accounts, does not process payments, and does not take custody of goods or cargo. Payment features are not active. See our Terms of Service.

2. Use of Google User Data

Google OAuth Data Disclosure:

  • What Data We Access: When you use Google Sign-In with Pipe Buyer, we request access to your Google email address, full name, and profile photo.
  • Why We Need It: We need this data solely to authenticate your identity, create your user account, and display your user profile within Pipe Buyer.
  • How We Store & Share It: Your Google data is stored securely using Firebase Authentication. Pipe Buyer does not share, sell, or rent your Google user data to any third parties, and does not use it for advertising or AI model training.

Pipe Buyer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. How We Use Information

  • Create and secure your account, and verify email and phone ownership.
  • Publish and manage your listings, wanted ads, offers, auctions, and dispatch jobs.
  • Deliver messages, notifications, and transaction updates.
  • Detect and review duplicate photographs, unsafe messages, fraud, and abuse.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and marketplace policies.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Keep records we are legally required to keep.
  • Diagnose faults and improve reliability.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

4. Moderation and Automated Processing

When you publish a listing, our servers calculate a SHA-256 fingerprint of each stored photograph and compare it against your other listings to detect exact reuse. Message content is screened by conservative automated safety signals.

Automated signals never penalise you on their own. They create a private case for a human reviewer. A person makes every moderation decision, you are notified of decisions that affect you, and you may appeal. Appeals are reviewed by a person.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Other users. Your public profile, your listings, and the content of messages you send are visible to the users you interact with. Exact dispatch locations and access notes are restricted to the parties awarded a job and are not shown on the public job board.

Service providers. We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, and App Check) to host and operate the service.

Legal. We may disclose information where required by law, to respond to a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Pipe Buyer, our users, or the public.

Business transfer. If Pipe Buyer is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

6. Where Your Information Is Stored

Production data is stored in Google Cloud data centres in the United States. If you are located in Canada, your information will be stored and processed in the United States and may be subject to lawful access requests under United States law.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Available to every user, directly in the application:

  • Access and export. Request a copy of your data. The export remains available for 7 days.
  • Correction. Edit your profile and listings directly.
  • Deletion. Request account deletion in Account settings. Deletion is scheduled with a 14-day grace period and can be cancelled during that window.
  • Session control. Review your device history and revoke active sessions.
  • Notifications. Push notifications are opt-in and can be turned off at any time.
  • Location. Grant or revoke device location permission at any time in your device settings.

Users in Canada may access and correct their personal information and may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Users in United States jurisdictions with applicable privacy legislation may have additional rights, including access, correction, deletion, and portability. Pipe Buyer does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

To exercise any right not available in the application, contact us using the details below.

9. Security

  • Firebase Authentication with verified email and phone ownership
  • Multi-factor authentication, required for all administrator accounts
  • Server-side authorisation — clients cannot directly write authoritative listing, offer, bid, dispatch, verification, or moderation state
  • Firestore and Cloud Storage security rules covering every role and file path
  • Firebase App Check to reject traffic from unauthorised clients
  • Rate limiting on account, listing, message, upload, offer, bid, and dispatch operations
  • Time-limited, single-purpose upload authorisations
  • Immutable audit records for administrative and moderation actions

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the applicable regulator as required by law.

10. Children

Pipe Buyer is an industrial marketplace intended for business use. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We will post any change to this policy with a new effective date. Where a change is material, we will ask you to review and accept the updated policy before you continue transacting.
