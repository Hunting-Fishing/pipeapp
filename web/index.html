Pipe Buyer

Buy & Sell Marketplace for Oil & Gas Products

Application Purpose & Identity: Pipe Buyer is an online B2B industrial marketplace, timed auctions platform, and freight trucking dispatch network for buying and selling oil & gas equipment, OCTG casing, tubing, line pipe, valves, and heavy machinery across the United States and Canada, with low seller fees. Pipe Buyer is a venue and is not a party to transactions between users.
