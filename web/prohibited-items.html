Prohibited Items Policy for Pipe Buyer

Effective Date: August 22, 2026
Last updated: August 22, 2026

This policy applies to listings, wanted ads, offers, auctions, Dispatch jobs, messages, attachments, and other content submitted through Pipe Buyer.

Core rule. You may list, request, advertise, transport, or transact only property and services that you are legally entitled to offer and that may lawfully be advertised, sold, purchased, possessed, transported, exported, imported, or transferred in the applicable jurisdiction.

1. Illegal, Stolen, Counterfeit, or Fraudulent Property

Pipe Buyer prohibits stolen property, property obtained by fraud, counterfeit goods, forged documents, altered serial-number or ownership records, cloned or falsified identification, and any item or service offered through material misrepresentation.

2. Weapons, Explosives, and Dangerous Devices

Firearms, ammunition, firearm parts intended to create or modify a weapon, explosives, explosive precursors offered for unlawful use, detonators, destructive devices, and other regulated weapons are prohibited unless Pipe Buyer later launches a separately reviewed and jurisdiction-controlled category. No such category is currently approved for Phase 1.

3. Controlled Substances and Contraband

Illegal drugs, controlled substances offered without lawful authority, drug-manufacturing equipment intended for unlawful use, illicit tobacco or nicotine products, smuggled goods, and other contraband are prohibited.

4. Hazardous Waste and Unsafe Materials

Hazardous waste, contaminated material, radioactive material, regulated chemical waste, or other dangerous material may not be listed or transported through Pipe Buyer unless the activity is lawful, the user has all required authority, and Pipe Buyer has expressly enabled the applicable category and workflow. Phase 1 does not provide a general hazardous-waste marketplace.

5. Regulated Property Rights and Restricted Transfers

Real property, mineral interests, royalty interests, leasehold interests, surface rights, securities, businesses sold as operating entities, or other regulated rights may not be published or transferred through Pipe Buyer unless a separately reviewed feature and jurisdiction pack has been enabled for that category. Phase 1 is focused on industrial equipment, materials, vehicles, portable buildings, related assets, and Dispatch services.

6. Property You Do Not Have Authority to Sell

You must not list leased, financed, consigned, employer-owned, government-owned, liened, jointly owned, abandoned, seized, or otherwise encumbered property unless you have the legal authority and any required consent to sell or transfer it. You are responsible for resolving title, lien, ownership, and release requirements before completing a transaction.

7. Unsafe or Deceptive Industrial Listings

Listings must not conceal known material defects, contamination, unsafe modifications, missing ownership documents, materially inaccurate specifications, false inspection claims, false hours or mileage, false pressure or grade information, or other facts that would materially mislead a reasonable buyer or carrier.

8. Circumvention and Evasion

You must not rename, split, bundle, disguise, miscategorize, or move prohibited content into messages or attachments to avoid platform controls. You must not use Pipe Buyer to arrange an unlawful transaction off-platform.

9. Dispatch Restrictions

Dispatch requests must describe lawful cargo and service requirements. Carriers remain responsible for authority, insurance, permits, dangerous-goods compliance, cargo securement, route legality, weight and dimension limits, and other transportation obligations. Pipe Buyer may reject cargo or routes that create legal, fraud, safety, or platform-risk concerns.

10. Enforcement

Pipe Buyer may block publication, remove content, restrict features, request ownership or compliance evidence, suspend accounts, preserve relevant audit records, or refer matters to appropriate authorities where required or reasonably necessary. Automated signals may create a review case; material enforcement decisions are subject to human review and appeal where an appeal process is provided.

11. Reporting

If you believe a listing, Dispatch job, message, or user violates this policy, use the in-app reporting tools or contact support@pipebuyer.com. Do not attempt to investigate suspected criminal activity yourself.

12. Changes to This Policy

Pipe Buyer may update this policy as categories, jurisdictions, and compliance controls change. A material change may require users to review and accept the current version before continuing with protected marketplace or Dispatch activity.
