Communications Policy for Pipe Buyer

Effective Date: August 22, 2026
Last updated: August 22, 2026

This policy applies to in-app messages, listing and Dispatch conversations, attachments, support correspondence, reports, appeals, notifications, and other communications sent through or about Pipe Buyer.

Keep business communication legitimate and verifiable. Do not use Pipe Buyer messaging to impersonate another person or company, pressure users into suspicious payments, collect credentials, distribute malware, conceal prohibited transactions, or evade fraud and safety controls.

1. Permitted Business Communications

Users may communicate about legitimate listings, wanted ads, offers, auctions, inspections, pickup and delivery, Dispatch quotes and awards, account support, disputes, and other approved Pipe Buyer workflows. Messages should be relevant to the transaction or service relationship.

2. Fraud, Impersonation, and Phishing

You must not impersonate Pipe Buyer, Stripe, another user, a carrier, a business, government authority, bank, insurer, or payment provider. Do not request passwords, one-time security codes, full payment-card data, banking credentials, or other authentication secrets through Pipe Buyer messages.

3. Suspicious Payment Requests

Do not falsely claim that Pipe Buyer requires payment by gift card, cryptocurrency, wire transfer, cash deposit, or another method that has not been presented through the approved product flow. Do not send fabricated invoices, payment confirmations, refund notices, escrow instructions, or links intended to imitate Pipe Buyer or an approved provider.

4. Harassment, Threats, and Abusive Conduct

Threats, harassment, discriminatory abuse, stalking, extortion, repeated unwanted contact, sexual exploitation, doxxing, or attempts to intimidate another user are prohibited. Legitimate commercial disagreement does not justify abusive conduct.

5. Spam and Unrelated Solicitation

Do not send bulk unsolicited messages, repetitive advertising, chain messages, deceptive lead generation, unrelated recruitment, or content that is materially unrelated to the applicable listing, Dispatch job, transaction, or support request.

6. Links and Attachments

Links and attachments must be relevant and safe. Malware, credential-stealing pages, executable payloads intended to compromise a device, deceptive shortened links, forged documents, or files used to facilitate fraud are prohibited. Pipe Buyer may restrict attachment types, sizes, destinations, or upload permissions.

7. Off-Platform Communication

Users may exchange contact information when reasonably necessary to complete legitimate business activity, but moving a conversation off-platform does not remove your obligations under the Terms or this policy. Pipe Buyer may have less ability to investigate communications that occur entirely outside the service.

8. Safety and Anti-Scam Practices

  • Verify the business, equipment, cargo, and counterparty before sending money or releasing property.
  • Be cautious when a user creates urgency, refuses reasonable verification, changes payment instructions unexpectedly, or asks you to bypass the normal transaction flow.
  • Do not rely on screenshots or forwarded messages as proof of payment; use provider-confirmed or otherwise independently verified payment evidence.
  • Report suspected account compromise, fraudulent links, or impersonation promptly.

9. Automated Safety Signals and Human Review

Pipe Buyer may use conservative automated signals to identify suspected fraud, unsafe messages, duplicate abuse patterns, malicious attachments, or account compromise. Signals may create a private review case. Material enforcement decisions are subject to human review and appeal where an appeal process is provided.

10. Notifications

Pipe Buyer may send transactional, security, account, listing, Dispatch, payment, moderation, and support notifications that are necessary to operate the service. Optional notifications may be controlled through available application, browser, device, or email preferences. Security, legal, or transaction-critical notices may still be sent where permitted and necessary.

11. Privacy and Records

Communications and related metadata are handled under the Pipe Buyer Privacy Policy. Pipe Buyer may retain messages, reports, appeals, moderation evidence, and transaction-related communications as reasonably necessary for service operation, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, safety, audit, and legal obligations.

12. Reporting and Enforcement

Use the in-app reporting tools or contact support@pipebuyer.com to report suspicious or abusive communications. Pipe Buyer may restrict messaging, remove content, suspend accounts, preserve evidence, or take other proportionate action where this policy or applicable law is violated.

13. Changes to This Policy

Pipe Buyer may update this policy as communication features, fraud controls, notification providers, and legal requirements change. A material change may require review and acceptance of the current version before protected Marketplace or Dispatch activity continues.
