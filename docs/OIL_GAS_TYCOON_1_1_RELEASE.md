## Implemented gameplay

### Used-equipment auctions

The Exchange now contains three rotating used-asset lots with condition, retail
comparison, bid amounts, leading bidder, and closing day. Rivals are simulated
companies in the local game; they act when the player advances a shift.

- A leading bid moves cash into escrow. Raising it holds only the additional
  amount. Bids cannot be withdrawn.
- Being outbid returns the full held amount. A winning lot delivers one asset
  to Fleet at its advertised condition, without charging the bid again.
- Escrow remains part of company net worth. Leading bids reserve fleet slots,
  and the treasury reserves room for refunds, including at its cash cap.
- Settlements and refunds occur once. Open lots refresh and recent results
  remain available in a bounded history. Save/reload preserves rival behavior.

### Research and technology

Company now has six technologies, each with three levels. One project may run
at a time. Funding is paid up front; the cost is its base cost multiplied by
the square of the target level. Levels one, two, and three require two, three,
and four staffed shifts respectively.

| Technology | Base funding | Benefit per completed level |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Production tuning | $18,000 | 5% more production potential, subject to operating constraints |
| Fleet dispatch | $14,000 | 10% more staffed transport capacity |
| Storage engineering | $12,000 | 15% more oil and gas storage |
| Preventive maintenance | $10,000 | Eight percentage points added to the repair discount |
| Drilling efficiency | $15,000 | 5% lower development cost after any drilling-rig discount |
| Market intelligence | $16,000 | One percentage point added to sale quotes, capped at the buy quote |

An employed engineer is required to start and advance research. Losing the
last engineer pauses progress without erasing completed shifts. Cancelling a
project loses that project's progress and does not refund funding. Company
restructuring cancels unfinished projects and forfeits auction deposits;
completed technology survives. The confirmation explains these consequences.

The interface places compact auction and research sections near the top of
Exchange and Company. It includes funding/bid validation, research progress and
pause explanations, recent auction outcomes, and updated career guidance.

## Existing saves and offline behavior

The game migrates original **schema 1** company data into **schema 2**, preserving
all existing company fields. Migrated companies receive initial auction lots
without consuming the old market random stream or changing the ID counter.
New research begins unlearned, with no project in progress.

The portable backup envelope remains `oil-gas-tycoon`, version `1`, and device
storage keys remain unchanged. New schema 2 saves require the updated game;
the original version 1.0 cannot read them. The previous-good-save recovery copy
remains available, and the first upgraded save retains the untouched legacy
backup. Export a backup before moving between installations or devices.

The simulation remains offline and single-player. No auctions, research, costs,
or deadlines advance while the game is closed. Web updates wait for existing
game tabs to close so each session uses a consistent set of files.

## Browser verification of the compiled release

The final web build was exercised in Chrome through the managed local preview.
It resumed the original `Frontier Energy` company on day 2 with $154,245 and
160 bbl, preserving its existing lease and crew. The following UI actions passed:

1. A $23,192 bid on a gas gathering skid deducted exactly that amount from
   available cash while leaving company value unchanged. The next shift's
   rival bid returned the full deposit and cleared the held balance.
2. Hiring an engineer cost $3,360 and added $280 to payroll. Storage engineering
   level 1 charged $12,000, showed one remaining shift after the first shift,
   and completed after the second staffed shift.
3. A drilling-rig auction settled once, using its held bid. The rig appeared in
   Fleet at the advertised 89% condition, with no second payment.
4. Completed storage research increased oil capacity from 1,500 to 1,725 bbl
   and gas capacity from 6,000 to 6,900 Mcf.
5. Reloading preserved day 4, $16,815 cash, 475 bbl, the three-person crew,
   the won rig at 89% condition, and the learned storage level.

These actions used a test company on the preview origin, separate from the
user's saved company on the hosted game's origin. This is browser validation;
it does not establish physical-device acceptance or repeat the prior release's
offline network-disconnection test.
