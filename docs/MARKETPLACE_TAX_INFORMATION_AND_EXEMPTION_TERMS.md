# Pipe Buyer Marketplace Tax Information, Exemption and Recovery Terms

**Policy version:** `2026-08-10-tax-responsibility-v1`  
**Operational tax policy:** `2026-08-10-ca-marketplace-tax-v1`

> **Implementation note:** This document is an operational/legal-control draft for Pipe Buyer and should be reviewed by qualified Canadian and B.C. tax/legal counsel before being represented as final legal advice. It deliberately does not claim that a private contract can eliminate a statutory tax obligation imposed directly on Pipe Buyer.

## 1. Purpose and scope

These terms apply to buyers, sellers, affiliates and business accounts using Pipe Buyer for marketplace listings, offers, purchases, sales, payments, external settlements, marketplace fees and tax-exemption requests.

Pipe Buyer may collect, validate, retain and use transaction, location, registration and exemption information to determine tax treatment, comply with tax-authority requirements, administer marketplace payments, maintain records and respond to audits or assessments.

## 2. User representations and continuing duty to update

Each buyer and seller represents, warrants and certifies that all information it supplies for tax purposes is complete, current and accurate, including:

- legal name and business identity;
- country, province/state and relevant business locations;
- delivery, pickup and destination facts;
- Canadian Business Number and GST/HST registration status;
- provincial sales-tax registrations, including B.C. PST numbers;
- whether a seller is registered under the normal GST/HST regime;
- the purpose for which purchased goods will be acquired and used;
- resale status and other exemption facts;
- exemption certificates, declarations, shipping documents and supporting evidence;
- whether information previously supplied has changed; and
- all other facts reasonably requested by Pipe Buyer to determine or document the tax treatment of a transaction.

A user must promptly update its Pipe Buyer tax profile when any supplied information becomes inaccurate, expires, is cancelled, or otherwise changes.

## 3. No self-approved tax exemption

Entering a tax registration number, checking an exemption box, or uploading a certificate does not by itself make a transaction tax exempt.

Pipe Buyer may require validation, administrator review, supporting records and transaction-specific approval. Pipe Buyer may decline an exemption, require additional evidence, or require tax to be collected whenever the available information is incomplete, inconsistent or not sufficient to support the exemption.

Where applicable law requires documentation at or before the time of sale, an exemption will not be treated as approved until the required information is recorded and the applicable Pipe Buyer review controls have passed.

## 4. Buyer and seller responsibility for incorrect information

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the buyer or seller whose false, incomplete, outdated, misleading or omitted information causes or contributes to an under-collection, non-collection, incorrect calculation, incorrect exemption, incorrect place-of-supply determination or other tax error is responsible for the resulting amount.

That responsibility includes, to the extent legally recoverable:

- unpaid GST/HST, PST, QST, RST, sales tax, VAT or other transaction tax;
- interest;
- penalties and assessments;
- refunds or credits that must be reversed;
- reasonable professional, audit and legal costs incurred to respond to the error;
- payment-provider or banking costs directly attributable to recovery; and
- other amounts that Pipe Buyer is legally required to pay because of the user's inaccurate or incomplete information.

## 5. Indemnification

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, each buyer and seller agrees to indemnify, reimburse and hold harmless Pipe Buyer and its operators from the amounts described above to the extent they arise from that user's breach of these terms, false statement, misleading omission, inaccurate exemption claim, invalid registration information, undisclosed change of status, or other inaccurate transaction fact supplied or certified by that user.

Where both buyer and seller supplied or certified inaccurate information, Pipe Buyer may allocate or pursue recovery from either or both to the extent permitted by applicable law and the facts of the transaction.

## 6. Statutory obligations are not disclaimed

Nothing in these terms excludes, transfers or limits a tax-collection, remittance, registration, reporting, recordkeeping or other obligation that applicable legislation imposes directly on Pipe Buyer.

If legislation treats Pipe Buyer as a collector, distribution platform operator, online marketplace facilitator, deemed supplier or other responsible person, Pipe Buyer will administer the transaction according to that law notwithstanding any agreement between the buyer and seller.

These terms are intended to allocate contractual responsibility for user-caused errors and preserve available statutory safe-harbour protections; they are not a representation that Pipe Buyer can contract out of tax legislation.

## 7. Good-faith reliance and verification

Pipe Buyer may rely on user-supplied statements and documents when permitted by law, but may verify registrations, request corroborating records and reject a claim when Pipe Buyer knows or reasonably should know that information may be false or insufficient.

Verification by Pipe Buyer is a compliance control and is not a guarantee that a tax authority will accept a registration, exemption or tax position.

## 8. Recovery, reserves, set-off and payout holds

To the fullest extent permitted by law and the applicable payment-provider rules, if Pipe Buyer reasonably determines that a tax amount may be owing because of a user-supplied error, Pipe Buyer may:

- create a tax-recovery obligation against the responsible account;
- place a compliance hold on marketplace activity or seller payouts;
- retain or establish a reasonable reserve from amounts otherwise payable to that user;
- set off a finalized tax-recovery obligation against future marketplace proceeds, commissions, refunds or other amounts payable by Pipe Buyer;
- invoice or otherwise request payment from the responsible buyer or seller;
- correct or reissue transaction records, invoices or tax documentation;
- suspend an exemption or tax-verification status while it is under review; and
- require further documentation before restoring payment or exemption privileges.

Pipe Buyer will not characterize a contingent amount as a finalized user debt until the underlying issue has been reasonably substantiated. Any automated recovery must remain subject to applicable law, payment-provider rules and the user's payment authorization.

## 9. Transaction-level tax record

Pipe Buyer may retain an immutable or append-only tax-compliance snapshot for each marketplace transaction, including the policy version, tax-profile revisions, seller registration status, exemption-claim identifier, evidence status, jurisdiction, tax calculation status and relevant review decisions.

Changing a profile after a transaction does not retroactively rewrite the transaction's historical tax-compliance snapshot. Corrections are recorded as subsequent events.

## 10. B.C. PST exemptions

For B.C. transactions, a PST number alone does not automatically exempt a purchase.

Depending on the exemption, Pipe Buyer may require a verified PST number, an applicable exemption certificate, an electronic declaration containing the required information, proof of resale, qualifying-use information, production machinery/equipment documentation, shipping records or other evidence required by B.C. law.

A user claiming an exemption certifies that the goods and intended use satisfy the exemption claimed. If the facts or intended use change, the user must notify Pipe Buyer and is responsible for any purchaser self-assessment or other obligation imposed by law.

## 11. Cross-border and multi-jurisdiction transactions

A transaction can involve more than one tax jurisdiction. A Canadian tax exemption or registration does not determine customs duties, import taxes, U.S. state sales tax, VAT, or other taxes outside that jurisdiction.

Pipe Buyer may require destination, origin, import/export and customer-tax information before enabling an international payment or exemption. Unsupported or uncertain jurisdictions may be held for manual tax review rather than automatically treated as tax free.

## 12. Cooperation with audits and authorities

Users must reasonably cooperate with Pipe Buyer in responding to a tax-authority inquiry concerning their marketplace activity, including by providing requested transaction facts, certificates and supporting records within a reasonable period.

Pipe Buyer may preserve and disclose tax records when required or permitted by applicable law, lawful process, tax-authority requirements or its privacy policy.

## 13. Corrections and refunds

A later verification, audit, exemption certificate or tax-authority determination may require the tax treatment of a transaction to be corrected. Pipe Buyer may issue or facilitate an appropriate tax refund, credit, supplemental charge or recovery process where legally permitted and operationally supported.

No user is entitled to a tax exemption or refund solely because a tax number or certificate was submitted after payment; the governing law and documentation timing rules control.

## 14. Acceptance and evidence

Pipe Buyer records acceptance of these terms by authenticated user ID, server timestamp and policy version. Where exemption certification is submitted electronically, Pipe Buyer may record the user's affirmative electronic acceptance together with the required declaration information as evidence of the certification.

## 15. Governing hierarchy

If these terms conflict with mandatory tax legislation, regulations or a binding tax-authority determination, the mandatory legal requirement controls. The remaining contractual allocation of responsibility continues to apply to the fullest extent permitted by law.
